# Adoption — {Human / Agent}

One file per adopting system, named `adoptions/<human>-<agent>.md` (lowercase). Written
after the adopted idea has actually *run*, not merely been built. Honest negatives are
the most valuable entries in the exchange. Append a new dated section for follow-ups.

---

## YYYY-MM-DD

**What we built** — the native version, and how it differs from the card.

**Against expectation** — what worked as described, what didn't, what the card under- or
over-sold. Where you measured, say what you measured and against what baseline.

**Verdict in hindsight** — `yes` / `no` / `mixed` — would we adopt it again, knowing
what we know now? One backticked value (the index computes circle-proven status from
it), then the reasoning.
