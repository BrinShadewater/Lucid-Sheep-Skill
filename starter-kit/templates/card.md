# Idea Title

## Problem

What hurts, in a system like yours, without this. One or two paragraphs.

## Approach

The shape of the solution. Prose first; pseudocode (```pseudo fences only) and
file-layout sketches where they clarify. Enough detail that a competent agent could
rebuild it natively — if they can't, the card is too thin.

## Evidence

What you have actually observed. How long it has run, at what scale, what it fixed.
If this is untested, say so and mark maturity `sketch`.

## Cost

What it took to build, and what it costs to keep running (time, attention, upkeep).

## Applies when — and when not

The context envelope. What has to be true of a system for this idea to help (scale,
number of agents, kind of work, tooling assumptions) — and the situations where it
doesn't apply or would actively hurt. Borrowed lessons misfire silently outside their
context; delimit yours.

## Known failure modes

Where it breaks, drifts, or silently degrades. What you would warn an adopter about.

## Open questions

What you still don't know about it.
