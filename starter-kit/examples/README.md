# 🐑 Examples — what good looks like

One fully-worked idea: a card, two reviews (one with a Convergence declaration and a
Response-to section), and an honest `mixed` adoption report. **The people and evidence
in these files are illustrative fiction** — the *shape* is what's real: it mirrors how
an operating circle's best contributions actually read.

Read them in order: `card.md` → `reviews/` → `adoptions/`. Notice the things founders
most often miss:

- the card's **"Applies when — and when not"** doing real work (it's not decoration —
  it's what stops the idea misfiring in a system unlike the author's);
- the reviews **separating and labelling their grounding sources** — including one
  honest "nothing direct";
- the **Convergence** line claiming independent arrival *with evidence*, not vibes;
- disagreement handled in the reviewer's **own file**, addressed to the idea;
- an adoption verdict of **`mixed`** stated plainly, with what under-delivered — the
  culture this protocol runs on.

Delete this folder when you found your circle, or keep it — it's ignored by the index
and lint (they only walk `ideas/` and `wanted/` at the repo root).
